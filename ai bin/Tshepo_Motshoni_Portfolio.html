Meet the owner

Tshepo Motshoni

The person behind Magaetswale Solutions — and the reason every job gets done right.

I'm Tshepo — founder of Magaetswale Solutions, a cleaning business built on one simple idea: your home should feel like a home, not a chore list.

I started this business because I saw too many people settle for rushed, careless cleaning. Every surface, every fabric, every corner — it all matters. I built a team that treats your space the way I'd treat my own.

From Pretoria East and beyond, we've delivered over 500 clean homes and grown a reputation for reliability, thoroughness, and respect. Whether it's a one-off deep clean or regular maintenance, we show up on time, do the work properly, and leave you with a space you're proud to live in.

Outside of cleaning, I'm passionate about building things — businesses, communities, and opportunities for South Africans. Magaetswale Solutions is part of that bigger picture.
